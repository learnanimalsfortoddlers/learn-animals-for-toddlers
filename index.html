Privacy Policy – Learn Animals for Toddlers

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Learn Animals for Toddlers is an educational animal-learning app designed for young children. The app uses animal pictures, animal sounds, spoken animal names and simple games.

Information and Accounts

The app does not require an account and does not ask users to enter names, email addresses, account details or other personal details.

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, photos, videos or personal files. It does not collect advertising identifiers, Android IDs or other persistent device identifiers.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, user-profiling or crash-reporting SDKs.

Local Game Data

The app stores limited game information locally on the device to remember progress and preferences. This includes settings and gameplay information such as music and voice preferences, difficulty, stars, eggs, babies, unlocked worlds and daily-reward information.

This information is stored in Android's private application storage. The app contains no implementation that transmits this game data to developer-operated servers or remote databases.

The app is configured to exclude its stored data from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Android may also temporarily retain game and screen state so that an interrupted session can be restored.

Text-to-Speech

The app can use the Text-to-Speech (TTS) service installed on the Android device to speak predefined game content such as animal names, quiz prompts and game messages.

The text supplied by the app for speech consists of predefined game content. It does not include user-entered text, personal details or saved progress totals.

Speech processing is performed by the installed TTS engine. The app does not enforce offline processing and does not determine the TTS engine's network use, retention or other data-handling behaviour.

Turning Voice off in Parent Settings stops spoken output. The app may still initialize the device's TTS service.

Internet, Advertising and Tracking

The app does not implement direct Internet communication and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or user-profiling functionality.

Children's Privacy

Learn Animals for Toddlers is designed for young children.

The app does not provide account creation, social networking, chat or user-generated-content features and does not ask children to provide personal details.

Diagnostic Logs

The app may write limited animal-playback and error diagnostic information to Android's system logs. The app does not implement uploading these logs.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and preferences remain in the app's private storage while the application is installed and are updated as the app is used.

Under normal Android behaviour, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its privately stored game progress and preferences. This does not describe deletion of Android system logs or information independently retained by a Text-to-Speech service.

The app has no user-account functionality.

Data Security

Game progress and preferences are stored using Android's private application storage. The app contains no implementation that transmits this information to developer-operated servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The "Last updated" date will be revised when this Privacy Policy is updated.
