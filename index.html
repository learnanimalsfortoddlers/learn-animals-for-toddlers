Privacy Policy — Learn Animals for Toddlers

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Learn Animals for Toddlers is a paid animal-learning game intended for children and their parents or guardians. It is provided by Learn Animals for Toddlers. For privacy questions, contact animalsoundskidssupport@gmail.com.

Personal information and advertising

The app does not collect or share personal information. It does not ask for names, email addresses, accounts, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or payment information. It contains no advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, tracking SDKs, or remote crash-reporting services. The app has no Internet permission and does not upload game data. Google Play handles the purchase separately under its own terms and privacy policy.

Information kept on your device

The app saves music and voice preferences, toddler mode, difficulty, stars, eggs, baby-animal totals, unlocked worlds, and the last daily-reward day in private storage on your device. Android may also retain the current screen and game-session state so play can resume after activity recreation. These records are used only to operate the game and are not sent to us. The app disables Android backup and excludes its data from cloud backup and device transfer.

The app also writes local diagnostic messages about playback, animal taps, and errors to Android's system log. It does not upload these logs or include names or other personal information in them.

Spoken guidance

Animal recordings and music are bundled with the app. Spoken names and guidance use the device's installed text-to-speech service. The app selects only English voices that the service reports as installed and usable without a network connection, preferring US English. It does not request voice downloads or fall back to a network-dependent voice. If an eligible voice is unavailable or the current speech request reports an error, further spoken guidance is skipped for the current app activity. When Voice is enabled, Parent Settings indicates that offline English speech is unavailable; when disabled, it displays Voice: OFF. Speech availability is checked again when the app activity is recreated. The game and bundled sounds remain available.

The app sends only game phrases to that on-device service, not personal information or saved game progress. The text-to-speech service is separate software supplied by your device or selected in Android settings. Its own operation and data practices are governed by its provider; the app cannot control that separate software's independent activity.

Removing local data

You can delete saved progress and settings by clearing the app's storage/data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This resets local progress. There is no app account or server-held game profile to delete. Android manages the retention of its system diagnostic logs separately.

Privacy-policy website

Opening this policy from Parent Settings requires answering a grown-up arithmetic question. After a correct answer, the app asks an external browser to open the policy website. It sends a fixed website address without attaching game progress, personal information, or identifiers. The browser and website host handle the resulting connection under their own privacy practices and may process standard connection information such as an IP address. The app does not operate an embedded browser or set cookies. Visiting the website is not required to play.

Children and changes to this policy

The app is designed for children without accounts, advertising, or collection of their personal information. Parents or guardians can use the contact above for questions. If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play declarations will be updated to reflect those changes.
